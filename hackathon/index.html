Submission Checklist

  • 3-minute max demo video link
  • Public GitHub repository
  • 200-350 word write-up
  • Team roster: names, grade/year, school
  • Selected track

Write-Up

  • Who are you helping?
  • What problem are you solving?
  • How does the AI work?
  • What STEM domain is involved?
  • What are the current limits?

Prompt

Build an AI-powered STEM application that makes a meaningful difference for a community you know.

Strong projects go beyond a basic chatbot wrapper. They show a real user, real STEM thinking, and a responsible AI component.

Tracks

  • Community Impact: local or regional community problems.
  • Education & Access: STEM learning or educational gaps.
  • Health & Environment: public health, biomedical, or environmental work.
  • Wildcard: original AI x STEM ideas.

Rules

  • Open to high school and undergraduate students.
  • Teams may have 1-3 members.
  • AI and a real STEM domain are required.
  • Credit open-source libraries and public APIs.
  • Judges and organizers may not compete.

Prizes

Recognition, mentorship, and visibility.

Top projects may receive an AI internship opportunity or direct referral, mentorship from KR3 and Minorities in STEM leadership, featured spotlights, network introductions, and participant recognition.
